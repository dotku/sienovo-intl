Summary of Technical Features and Industry Application Solutions for Mainstream Industrial Embedded Platforms (RK/ZYNQ/Jetson/TI/NXP/AM62X)

**Abstract**: The fields of industrial control, edge intelligence, and high-end equipment are rapidly iterating. Traditional single-ARM embedded platforms can no longer meet the complex requirements of low-latency motion control, high-precision data acquisition, AI edge inference, and hard real-time industrial control. This article focuses on six mainstream hardware architectures in the industrial sector: Rockchip RK3588/RK3576/RK3568, ZYNQ/MPSoC, Jetson, TI, AM62X, and NXP. It deeply analyzes ten core technical capabilities: **ARM, FPGA, ARM+FPGA, FPGA+AI, real-time systems, EtherCAT, low-latency control, machine vision, Codesys industrial control, and edge inference**. Combining these with six core industrial scenarios—**semiconductor equipment, power storage, robotics, rail transit, marine, and CNC machine tools**—this article systematically compares the technical advantages, suitable scenarios, development essentials, and implementation challenges of each platform, providing a comprehensive reference
