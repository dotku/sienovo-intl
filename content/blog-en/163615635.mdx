Zynq MPSoC Semiconductor High-Precision Motion Control Solution | FPGA Hardware Interpolation for Sub-Micron Servo Real-time Control

**Tags: Zynq MPSoC, ARM+FPGA, Semiconductor Equipment, Motion Control, Hardware Interpolation, Servo Control, Sub-Micron Accuracy, Domestic Equipment**

**Abstract:** Core equipment in semiconductor die bonding, wire bonding, wafer handling, and probe testing demands extreme precision in motion positioning, trajectory smoothness, and start-stop response speed. Sub-micron positioning errors and trajectory jitters can directly lead to chip misalignment, poor wire bonding, and wafer fragmentation. Traditional pure ARM motion solutions rely on software interpolation and pulse output, suffering from low refresh rates, trajectory jitter, start-stop overshoot, and poor multi-axis synchronization. X86 industrial PC + external motion control card solutions are complex to wire, costly, and have uncontrollable bus delays, making them unsuitable for high-end semiconductor precision motion scenarios. This article, based on the Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC heterogeneous architecture, builds an FPGA hardware high-speed motion control system.
